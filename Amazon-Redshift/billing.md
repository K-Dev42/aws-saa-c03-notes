### Compute Node Hours
- The total number of hours ran across all nodes in the bliing period
- Billed for 1 unit per node, per hour
- Not charged for leader node hours, only compute nodes incur charges

### Backup
- Backup are stored in S3 and you are billed the S3 storage fees

### Data transfer
- Billed for only transfer within a VPN, not outside of it
