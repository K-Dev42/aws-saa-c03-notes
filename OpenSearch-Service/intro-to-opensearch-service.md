## Definition
Amazon OpenSearch Service is a managed full-text search service that makes it easy to deploy, operate, and scale OpenSearch, a popular open-source search and analytics engine 

Two engines can be deployed: OpenSearch and ElasticSearch

What is the ELK stack?
- Stands for Elasticsearch, Logstash, and Kibana are three services commonly used together
    1. Elasticsearch: full-text search and analytics engine
    2. Logstash: data processing pipeline
    3. Kibana: visualization layer for stored data
