## Stateless Firewalls
These do not aware the state of the request. In both direction they'll treat you like a stranger and stop you both ways and do a rule check. In other words, they will check all traffics that goes through them. 


## Stateful Firewalls
These firewalls are aware the state of the requests. When it comes to SGs they allow all outbound request, responses for requests that originated outbound are allowed back through
