## Definition
AWS Snow Family are **storage and compute devices used to physically move data in or out of the cloud** when moving data over the internet or private connection when the connection is too slow, difficult or costly.

These are actual hardware we used to carry in the real world.

Hardware list:
- Snowcone (comes in two sizes)
    - 8 TB of Storage (HHD)
    - 14 TB of Storage (SSD)

- Snowball Edge (comes in two types)
    - Storage optimized
        - 80 TB
        - 210 TB
    - Compute Optimized
        - 39.5 TB

- Snowmobile: 100 PB of storage

> Data is delivered to Amazon S3
